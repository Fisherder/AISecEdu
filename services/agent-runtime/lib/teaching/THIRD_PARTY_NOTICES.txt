Adaptation: direct statements, concrete subjects and relationships, removal of
filler and inflated claims, coherent paragraph rewriting, stable terminology,
genre-specific voice, and an independent multi-criterion check. Educational
content retains technical definitions, meaningful questions, exact quantities,
conditions, uncertainty, citations and normal Chinese punctuation. No invented
facts, personal experience, forced slang, or claims of AI-detector evasion.
Blanket bans on adverbs, passive voice, questions, lists of three and dashes were
not adopted. Some upstream example rewrites add unsupported facts or drop scope;
those examples were not adopted. Our examples concern specific teaching contexts
and must not be copied as factual content for unrelated topics.
